About Our Company

A Sacred Tribute to Preserve Memories for Generations

Machaneng Tombstones Proprietary Limited is a locally owned memorial craftsmanship company established to provide families in Machaneng and the greater surrounding region with dignified, reliable, and heartfelt memorial solutions.

Founded on compassion, integrity, and respect, our company understands that a tombstone is not simply a stone — it is a sacred tribute, a permanent symbol of love, and a place where memories are preserved for generations. We are committed to walking with families during their time of loss and ensuring that every memorial reflects the life, values, and legacy of their loved one.

Our Mission

Deliver high-quality memorial products that honour the departed with dignity, empathy, and long-lasting workmanship.

Our Vision

To become the most preferred and trusted tombstone provider across Botswana, known for service excellence and community commitment.

End-to-End Support

Our Comprehensive Services

From preliminary material selection to installation and after-care, Machaneng Tombstones walks with you through every single step.

1. Custom Tombstone Design

We collaborate closely with families to create personalized tombstone designs. Whether traditional, modern, or symbolic, our design process captures the individuality and story of the departed.

2. Material Selection Guidance

We provide a variety of premium-grade materials, including high-quality granite and marble in multiple colors and finishes. Our team guides clients in selecting the ideal material for longevity, beauty, and budget.

3. Professional Engraving & Lettering

Our advanced engraving ensures clear, deep, and lasting inscriptions. We assist with font selection, epitaph writing, portrait engraving, religious symbols, and custom artwork.

4. Installation & Cemetery Placement

We deliver full installation services at local cemeteries, including foundation preparation, secure placement of the tombstone, alignment, and thorough site clean-up for a seamless experience.

5. Maintenance & After-Care

We offer guidance on cleaning, maintaining, and protecting the tombstone to preserve its appearance and structural integrity for many years.
